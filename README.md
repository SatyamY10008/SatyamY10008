## About Me

I work on data analysis, forecasting, and reporting workflows in the solar energy domain. My current work involves processing weather station and solar plant datasets, transforming time-series data, working with SQL Server, and building reporting workflows to make operational data easier to analyze and use.

I use Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel, and Power Query to clean and transform data, prepare datasets for analysis, and support forecasting and report generation. I also work with time-interval data, including converting one-minute measurements into fifteen-minute intervals and organizing data from multiple sources.

My background is in Electronics and Communication Engineering, which gives me a foundation in instrumentation, sensors, and electrical systems. I am building on that foundation through practical experience with solar energy data, analytics, and reporting systems.
